## Originality and attribution

The new contribution is the specific organizational protocol, executable failure
fixtures and traceable evaluation. Attenuation, authorization, hierarchy, revocation
and audit are established ideas. No priority claim is made for execute as a term.
Concepts from the supplied Alejandro context notes inform the framing; no claim is
made that every analogy in those notes is original or experimentally supported.

Agent Delegate's prose and experimental records are not copied into this project.
Its approach to a protected direct channel is explicitly cited. Its local source
is at `../agent-delegate/report/latex/main.tex`; provenance hashes are recorded in
`data/local-provenance.json`. The supplied notes and template are hashed there too.
Only the freely licensed Old Standard font files were copied as build assets;
their OFL notice is included. This project's LaTeX style is a transparent adaptation
of the same template roles and typography, not an official Apart LaTeX class.

## Submission fit

Suggested track: Open Track, with an operational containment artifact. No form has
been submitted. The report follows the official template's section roles and uses
Letter portrait with one-inch margins and 11-point text. Typography is adapted in
LaTeX; the template permits structural adaptation. It includes an abstract, related
work, reproducible methods, results, limitations, conclusion, code/data statement,
references, and Limitations and Dual-Use Considerations appendix.

Maximum body length is eight pages, excluding references and appendices. The build
records the actual body and total counts. Public repository and video are optional. The project is published at
https://github.com/mpodeley/governing-execute-sprint-2026 with a static project page
and downloadable release. No video or sprint submission is claimed. Final author review is pending;
the LLM statement describes assistance and automated verification accurately.
